Monkey D. Luffy

About Luffy

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates. His dream is to become the Pirate King and find the One Piece. He is cheerful, brave and cares deeply about his friends.

Luffy's Devil Fruit

Luffy ate the Devil Fruit that gave his body rubber-like properties. Its true name is Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika. This power allows Luffy to use his body in creative ways.

My Favorite Character

Luffy is one of my favorite anime characters because he is funny, brave and never gives up on his dreams.
